# Dynopay Logo & Brand Mark Redesign

A new Dynopay logo system — a geometric symbol paired with a custom wordmark — built to hold its own next to Coinbase, Stripe, Bybit and Kraken.
The mark expresses Dynopay's core story: crypto payments flowing straight into the merchant's own wallet (non-custodial, direct settlement).

## Who it's for
- Prospective merchants meeting the brand for the first time on the marketing site.
- Logged-in merchants who see the mark every day in the dashboard, checkout and emails.
- Every small-surface context where a brand has to survive: favicon, app/PWA icon, social shares, and partner/press placements.

## Core features and experience
- **A symbol/mark.** Geometric, built on a strict grid, legible from a 16px favicon up to a billboard, and usable as an app icon. The idea it carries is *payment flow → settlement*: value moving in one clear direction into a held/owned form (an arrow/chevron/aperture motif), designed so it can also read as a "D" where that feels natural rather than forced. Single gold accent on a dark base; no gradients, no 3D, no decorative flourish.
- **A custom wordmark.** "Dynopay" set in a geometric sans with subtly customised letterforms (tuned apertures, x-height and spacing) so it does not look like an off-the-shelf font, and verified to render cleanly with European accented characters for the five locales.
- **Lockups.** Horizontal (mark + wordmark), stacked, mark-only, and wordmark-only — so the brand fits wide nav bars, square tiles and tight corners alike.
- **Colour variants.** Gold-on-dark (primary), dark-on-light, and full monochrome (solid black / solid white) for compliance, print and single-colour contexts.
- **Concept exploration first.** 2–3 distinct concept directions for the mark are presented up front as quick visuals; one is chosen before any polishing happens.
- **Full deliverable set.** Production vector logo files, a multi-size favicon, a PWA/app icon, a social/OG share image, and a one-page usage note (clear space, minimum size, do/don't).
- **Wired into the live product.** The new logo replaces the current one across the marketing nav, auth pages, dashboard, loading states, footer, checkout, and the browser tab / installed-app icon — adapting automatically between light and dark surfaces.

## User flow
- **Choosing the logo:** review 2–3 concept directions → pick the winner → it is refined into the full system → it ships.
- **Encountering it in the product:** a visitor sees the mark + wordmark in the marketing nav; it carries through the auth screens and into the dashboard; it appears mark-only as the favicon, app icon and in tight spaces; it anchors the checkout and transactional context; and it switches cleanly between the light and dark areas of the site already in place.

## UI/UX feel
Institutional reliability at the core, digital-first restraint in the execution. Geometric precision and symmetry signal stability and trust, with a subtle lean of forward motion. High contrast throughout (meets accessibility contrast standards), gold held back as the single accent on a dark neutral base — consistent with the calmer gold/dark system already used across the site and the recently reworked auth trust panel. Nothing in the mark exists that does not serve a structural or communicative purpose.

## Implementation phases

**Phase 1 — built now (MVP).**
Explore 2–3 concept directions for the mark; on the chosen direction, produce the primary logo system — symbol + custom wordmark, the four lockups, and the three colour variants — as production vector files. Generate the favicon (multiple sizes), the PWA/app icon, and a social/OG share image. Replace the current logo across the live surfaces (nav, auth, dashboard, loading, footer, checkout, browser tab / app icon) with light/dark adaptation, and include a one-page usage note.

**Phase 2 — later.**
Branded email header and receipt/transactional treatment; a small logo motion (entrance / loading micro-animation); and localised wordmark checks across de, es, fr, nl and pt.

**Phase 3 — later.**
A full brand guide (clear-space and colour tokens, misuse examples, co-branding rules), a downloadable partner/press kit, a merchant-facing "Powered by Dynopay" badge, and an optional motion/3D variant for hero and social use.

## Assumptions
- Logo architecture is **symbol/mark + wordmark**, not wordmark-only or a plain "D" monogram.
- The mark's idea is **payment flow → direct settlement** (value moving straight to the merchant's wallet), allowed to read as a "D" only where it stays natural.
- Palette keeps **gold (#FFD100) as the single accent on dark**, plus a dark-on-light variant and a monochrome variant; no new accent colour is introduced.
- The name is kept as **"Dynopay"** (one word); exact casing in the wordmark is settled during design.
- **2–3 concept directions are shown first**, then one is refined and shipped (rather than committing to a single direction sight-unseen).
- Rollout is the **full set** (logo + favicon + app icon + OG image) wired across nav, auth, dashboard, checkout, footer and the browser tab.
- The shipped logo is **scalable vector**; concept exploration may use generated raster visuals, but the final assets are vector.
- The existing calmer gold/dark design system and the new auth trust panel remain the **reference for consistency**.
- This covers brand/logo visuals only; it does not change product functionality, copy, or the underlying payment flows.
