# SafeDeal Logo Re-imagined

SafeDeal gets one new logo family, replacing the three versions in use today: the gold shield with arrows on the website, the shield with a tick in link previews, and plain text in emails.
Three research-based ideas are presented side by side. The chosen one then replaces every SafeDeal logo across the site, tab icon, app icons, emails, share images and PDFs.

## Who it's for
- **The owner**, who picks the direction and wants one consistent SafeDeal identity.
- **SafeDeal buyers and sellers**, who meet the logo in emails, deal links shared in chats, the browser tab and receipts. For them the mark has to signal "money is held safely until the deal is done".

## Core features and experience
**Research basis (summary)**
- Escrow competitors use a navy wordmark with a green tick (Escrow.com) or a blue lettermark (Trustap). SafeDeal stands apart with black and white plus one gold accent.
- 2026 fintech advice: avoid clichés such as shields, locks and padlocks. Use one simple symbol designed to work at 16 px first. Ship it as a family (symbol, full logo, black-only, white-only) that works in light and dark mode.
- The escrow idea to express: **two sides, money held in the middle, released when both are done.**

**Three concepts to choose from**
1. **The Hold**: an "S" built from two interlocking halves (buyer and seller), with a small gold square held at the centre (the money in escrow).
2. **The Seal**: two arcs overlapping into one closed ring, like a handshake seen as a seal of agreement. Gold marks the point where the arcs join.
3. **The Release**: a bold "S" whose last stroke becomes a tick, with the tick in gold (deal complete, funds released).

Each concept is shown in real use:
- Full logo (symbol + "SafeDeal" wordmark) on light and on dark
- Symbol alone, plus the tab icon at actual 16 px and 32 px on light and dark tabs
- Home-screen / app icon tile
- Email header in light and dark mode
- Link-preview card, as seen when a deal link is shared in WhatsApp or Telegram

**Wordmark**
- "SafeDeal" in one colour: black on light, white on dark. The current two-tone version with a gold "Deal" is retired.
- Clean, modern sans-serif with tuned spacing, so it reads as its own brand.

**Rollout of the chosen logo (everywhere SafeDeal shows a logo)**
- Website: header, footer, sign-in, deal pages, wallet, mobile menu, loading and success screens
- Browser tab icon that adapts to the tab theme (black on light tabs, white on dark tabs), plus a legacy fallback
- Home-screen / app icons (Apple, Android, installable app)
- Emails: black logo on white in light mode, switching automatically to white in dark mode. It is built so it stays readable when Gmail forces its own dark mode.
- Link-preview images: the SafeDeal site card and the per-deal share cards
- PDF documents: deal summaries, top-up receipts and statements
- Admin panel places that show the SafeDeal brand
- An image file of the new logo, sized for the Telegram alerts bot's profile picture

## User flow
1. The owner opens a private comparison page on the preview site (not linked for customers). It shows the 3 concepts in every context above, light and dark.
2. The owner picks one, or asks for tweaks such as weight, spacing or how much gold.
3. The chosen logo is finalised as a full family (symbol, full logo, black-only, white-only, icon tiles).
4. It replaces every SafeDeal logo listed above. The old shield versions are removed.
5. A final review shows the site, tab icon, an email in light and dark, and a shared deal link with the new logo.

## UI/UX feel
- Calm, precise and confident, in line with the redesigned emails: lots of white space and solid shapes, no gradients, no 3D, no glow.
- Gold is a single small accent inside the symbol, never the whole logo.
- The symbol is designed to read at 16 px first and scaled up after that, never the other way round.

## Implementation phases
**Phase 1 (MVP, built now)**
- Research summary plus the 3 concepts, shown on the private comparison page
- After the owner's pick: final logo family and rollout to every surface listed above
- Old SafeDeal logos removed; new tab icon and share images refreshed so browsers and chat apps pick them up

**Phase 2**
- SafeDeal brand page and downloadable press kit (logo files plus simple usage rules: spacing, minimum size, do's and don'ts)
- Subtle animated logo for the site's loading and "deal funded" moments
- Social media kit (profile images, banners)

**Phase 3**
- Wider SafeDeal identity: illustration and icon style, marketing templates, stickers and merch
- Landing-page visuals refreshed around the new symbol

## Assumptions
- SafeDeal keeps its own warmer gold (#FFC61A), distinct from Dynopay's yellow, as the single accent colour.
- Emails use the gold-free black/white logo, keeping the earlier rule that gold appears only on the main email button. The website, app icons and share images use the version with the gold accent.
- The tab icon is black on light tabs and white on dark tabs; the gold accent stays only if it is still clear at 16 px.
- "Fully independent" means no Dynopay name, mark or "by Dynopay" badge appears with the SafeDeal logo anywhere. Any existing one next to the logo is removed. Legally required operator lines in footers stay as they are configured today.
- The name stays "SafeDeal"; only the visual identity changes.
- The logos are drawn as precise vector shapes, not AI-generated images, so they stay sharp at every size. AI image tools may be used for early mood exploration only.
- The Telegram bot's profile picture has to be uploaded by hand through Telegram's BotFather, so a ready-sized file is provided rather than set automatically.
- The comparison page is temporary and is removed once a concept is chosen and rolled out.
- Nothing changes on the Dynopay side: its logo, emails and favicon stay as just redesigned.
