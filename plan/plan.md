# SafeDeal brand refresh + Dynopay title localization — Plan

## What this covers
Six changes, in two groups:

**SafeDeal (safedeal.sh) brand refresh**
1. New yellow-and-black visual identity
2. A new, better SafeDeal logo
3. A SafeDeal-specific favicon (fixes the Dynopay icon showing in the tab)
4. A new, more impressive 3D/animated landing page
5. Copy rewrite so SafeDeal owns escrow, custody, disputes and payouts, and Dynopay is named only as the payment provider

**Dynopay (dynopay.com)**
6. Every page title (browser tab) shown in the visitor's language across all supported languages

## Scope boundary (please confirm)
- Items 1–5 change **SafeDeal only**. Dynopay's own look, logo, favicon and marketing stay exactly as they are.
- SafeDeal stays **English-only**. The title-translation work (item 6) is **Dynopay-only**, matching "escrow stays in English for now."
- Supported languages for Dynopay titles: English, Portuguese, French, Spanish, German, Dutch.

---

## 1. Yellow-and-black identity (SafeDeal)
Today SafeDeal uses indigo + dark navy with small amber touches. This replaces that with a yellow-and-black scheme across the SafeDeal landing, sign-in, deal pages, wallet, help and legal pages, and buttons/links/highlights.

Proposed direction (adjustable):
- **Black-dominant**, premium feel: near-black hero and headers, white/soft-grey content sections, **yellow as the single strong accent** (primary buttons, the logo, links, active states, progress/goal highlights).
- Proposed yellow: a confident golden-yellow (not neon), tuned so black text on yellow and yellow on black both stay easily readable.

Decisions worth challenging:
- **a.** Yellow shade / mood — golden (warm, trustworthy) vs. bright lemon (louder, more energetic).
- **b.** Overall balance — black-dominant (as proposed) vs. mostly-light pages with yellow-and-black accents only.

## 2. New SafeDeal logo
Today's logo is a generic shield-with-checkmark in an indigo box next to the word "SafeDeal." A new, distinctive mark + wordmark is created in the yellow/black identity and used everywhere the brand appears (header, footer, favicon, emails, share images), delivered so it stays crisp at every size.

Decisions worth challenging:
- **a.** Design a fresh logo (assumed), or you supply an existing SafeDeal logo file.
- **b.** Mark concept (assumed: a modern symbol of safe exchange — e.g. shield / lock / handshake / check motif). Alternatives can be shown before it's finalized.

## 3. SafeDeal favicon
Right now the SafeDeal browser tab, bookmark and phone home-screen icon all show Dynopay's icon. SafeDeal gets its own yellow/black favicon (derived from the new logo) on every SafeDeal page. Dynopay's favicon is unchanged.

## 4. New 3D / animated landing page (SafeDeal)
Goal: a landing page that feels markedly more modern and "alive" than today's static one — depth, motion, and a clear animated illustration of how escrow protects both sides.

- **Kept (re-skinned + animated):** the four-step "how it works," the live fee calculator, the fees + cancellations/disputes section, the FAQ, and the calls-to-action.
- **Added:** a 3D hero with motion, plus tasteful scroll-reveal animation through the page.
- **Accessibility:** motion is reduced automatically for visitors who ask their device for reduced motion; a lighter static version is shown on low-power phones so the page never feels slow.

Decision worth challenging:
- **a.** Fidelity — a **true interactive 3D hero** (most impressive, heavier to load) vs. **lighter 3D-style motion** (layered/animated, fastest on older phones). Proposed default: interactive 3D hero **with** an automatic lighter fallback on mobile/low-power devices, so it looks premium without hurting load time.

## 5. Copy rewrite — who does what
Today's SafeDeal copy credits Dynopay with holding the money, paying out, and deciding disputes (e.g. "Held in USDT by Dynopay," "Powered by Dynopay — payments, custody and payouts," "Dynopay decides… as a last resort").

New positioning across all SafeDeal wording:
- **SafeDeal** is the escrow service: it holds the buyer's funds, runs the deal, resolves disputes, and releases/refunds/pays out.
- **Dynopay** is named only as the **payment provider** that processes the crypto payment (e.g. "payments processed by Dynopay"), and nothing more.

Decisions worth challenging:
- **a.** Confirm this is **positioning/wording only** — the underlying way funds actually move does not change; only how it's described to users.
- **b.** Confirm SafeDeal should be presented as the party that **holds funds and arbitrates disputes**.

## 6. Dynopay page titles in every language
Every Dynopay page's browser-tab title and description will appear in the visitor's selected language for all six supported languages. Any titles that are currently missing a translation, or that show in English regardless of language, are corrected. SafeDeal/escrow titles stay in English.

## Assumptions (will proceed on these unless you say otherwise)
- The rebrand, logo, favicon, landing and copy changes touch SafeDeal only; Dynopay is left as-is.
- The new SafeDeal logo is designed fresh (concepts shown before finalizing) unless you provide one.
- Yellow/black means a black-dominant look with a golden-yellow accent (adjustable per your answer to 1a/1b).
- The 3D landing uses an interactive 3D hero with an automatic lighter fallback on mobile/reduced-motion.
- The copy change is positioning-only; money mechanics are unchanged.
- SafeDeal transactional emails are re-skinned to match the new identity for consistency. Say if that should be out of scope.

## The short list of decisions
1. Yellow shade + black-dominant vs. accent-only (1a/1b)
2. Design the logo fresh, or you supply one (2a)
3. 3D fidelity: full interactive 3D hero vs. lighter motion (4a)
4. Confirm SafeDeal holds funds + arbitrates, Dynopay = payments only, wording-only (5a/5b)
5. Include SafeDeal emails in the reskin — yes/no
